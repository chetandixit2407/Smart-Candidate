             partners,  developers,  vendors  and  business  operations.  You  acknowledge  that  such  access  is 
             provided strictly for authorised Company work and is subject to the following obligations.

             “Confidential  Information”  includes,  without  limitation,  client and investor names, phone numbers,

             considered,  budgets,  transaction  values,  booking  or  payment  information,  portfolio  information, 
             investor classification, CRM records, lead databases, cold data, buyer data, spreadsheets, reports,
